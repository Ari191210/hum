# Hum

**A free touchless switch for people who can't speak and can't reliably use a touchscreen, built from the speaker and microphone of the Android phone their family already owns.**

The phone plays an inaudible ultrasonic tone and listens to the echo off the user's hand. A deliberate movement shifts the echo's pitch slightly (the Doppler effect), and that movement acts as a "switch" on a scanning communication board that speaks phrases aloud.
